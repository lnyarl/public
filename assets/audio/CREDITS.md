# Audio credits

These recordings are bundled locally. Gameplay never requests audio from an external host.
All selected recordings are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Files | Creator | Original release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `meadow-thoughts.ogg` | Écrivain | [Meadow Thoughts — solo harp, 2:29](https://opengameart.org/content/meadow-thoughts) |
| `page-turn.ogg`, `seal-close.ogg`, `coins.ogg`, `steel.ogg` | Kenney / Kenney Vleugels | [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) |
| `click.ogg`, `bell.ogg`, `chimes.ogg`, `drum.ogg`, `warning.ogg`, `danger.ogg`, `loss.ogg`, `error.ogg` | Robin Lamb; samples from Versilian Studios' VCSL and VSCO 2 CE libraries | [UI Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sound-effects-button-clicks-user-feedback-notifications) |

Source filenames, URLs, sizes and SHA-256 checksums are preserved in `sources.json`. Original audio bytes are unchanged; playback adds gain control and fades. Kenney's supplied license is preserved in `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.

The original synthesized score and cues in `src/audio.js` remain available when a recording cannot load or decode.
